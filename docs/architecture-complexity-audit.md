# OpalFusion Architecture Complexity Audit

Status date: 2026-08-14. Audit base: private `draft` at `a8975c44e1152fee527e0657c5631ecec7d3de59`. This report records the original outbound-authority cleanup plus three bounded Mosaic ownership follow-ups; it is not a protocol revision, deployment plan, or live-mainnet readiness claim.

## Verdict

The package direction is sound: OpalCrypto owns reusable cryptographic computation, OpalFusion owns collaborative protocol state and transport semantics, and OpalBase owns wallet reservation, signing, persistence, approval, and broadcast. The dominant complexity is internal to the private Mosaic scaffold, where security boundaries were added faster than obsolete test and constructor seams were removed. The completed slices remove the alternate outbound route authority and reservation-only execution branch, consolidate duplicated route allocation without merging purpose policy, make `AdmissionLedger` the sole raw acknowledgement-set owner, and keep the expensive validation lane explicit. CashFusion behavior and public APIs are unchanged.

## Measured Baseline and Result

The original outbound-authority baseline contained 439 production Swift files and 40,552 production lines, including 188 Mosaic files and 26,060 Mosaic lines. Tests contained 306 Swift files and 61,199 lines. That cleanup left 40,436 production lines and 61,272 test lines before this report: production decreased by 116 lines while tests grew by 73 lines to construct the real attempt owner instead of bypassing it and to keep malformed mailbox projections covered at that owner boundary. The meaningful reduction was one route-authority enum, one raw dependency initializer, one raw bridge initializer, two bridge-only mailbox errors, and every direct provider branch. Those measurements predate the separately bounded roadmap follow-ups below.

## Ownership Map

| Concern | Authoritative owner | Preserved boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CashFusion protocol execution | `Client.Session`, primary/covert runtimes, and the round engine | Host reservation and transaction assembly remain injected wallet capabilities. |
| Mosaic phase semantics | Attempt/runtime reducers and the selected contributor or conductor coordinator | The generic mainnet-alpha runtime driver remains disabled. |
| Mosaic admission and replay | `AdmissionLedger` plus the accepted-fact journal seam | Only state-consuming authenticated admission is journaled before effects. |
| Outbound route access and claims | One `PostManifestAttemptTransportOwner` per peer and attempt | The owner is the bridge's sole validation gateway; the injected provisioner remains authoritative for raw connections and isolation leases. |
| Outbound publication lifecycle | Control/anonymous bridges, purpose-specific batch publishers, and one-shot relay publishers | Exact attempt, material, recipient, three-route, two-acknowledgement, cancellation, and closure checks remain layered at their semantic boundaries. |
| Inbound fan-in | Attempt owner provisioning, fan-in, ingress, and the specialized driver | One claim authorizes construction; fan-in owns startup, bounded serialization, source loss, and drain. |
| Wallet and broadcast | OpalBase host contracts | OpalFusion exposes no broadcast callback and cannot move BCH. |
| Cryptography | OpalCrypto | OpalFusion retains domain translation and protocol ordering, not key storage or wallet authority. |

## Applied Findings

1. **P1 resolved — alternate contributor route access.** The contributor bridge previously accepted either an attempt owner or direct raw route providers and mailbox values. Only tests used the direct path, but it bypassed the owner's cross-purpose connection and isolation-lease claims. The bridge now derives recipients and both provider closures from one nonoptional owner, while the injected provisioner remains the raw route-capability authority. Tests construct representative owners, and invalid mailbox projections remain covered at the owner boundary before provisioning.
2. **P2 resolved — validation-process ownership.** The slow RSA filter now includes every known suite that reaches real authorization evaluators: runtime, admission, conductor, contributor, reservation coordinator, local BCH-signature, anonymous publication bridge, anonymous batch publisher, contributor conformance, contract, and Opal v0 authorization. The full and Mosaic wrappers no longer start the contract suite in a separate evaluator-paying process.
3. **P2 resolved — focused feedback contract.** `./scripts/run-validation-loop.sh mosaic-fast` statically rejects evaluator access, evaluator generation, and real-material fixture preparation in its selected sources, then runs only pure route-plan validation and contributor lifecycle coverage. The larger RSA-free composition/provisioning/gate aggregate is intentionally not called the fast lane.

## Roadmap Findings and Reopen Signals

1. **P2 resolved — reservation execution dependency.** The coordinator now has one nonoptional execution dependency and validates complete actual-versus-material lease equality through one stateless concrete validator before publication. The material-construction-free `MosaicMainnetAlphaReservationCoordinatorDependencyValidator` proves exact lease acceptance, foreign-reference and changed-reservation rejection, unconditional material-dependency invocation, exact pre-sign release, and exact-reference recovery after release failure. Its static guard rejects evaluator access or generation, execution-fixture preparation, `LocalContributionMaterial.build`, and material-fixture construction. The existing full-material reservation suite runs once inside the serialized RSA-dependent process, while `MosaicMainnetAlphaContributorExecutorValidator` retains successful execution, pre-sign release, and post-sign recovery coverage without creating a second publication authority.
2. **P2 resolved — duplicated control and anonymous route-allocation mechanics.** Control and anonymous publication now share one concrete `PostManifestPublicationRouteAllocation` for deterministic requests, complete recipient indexing, and connection-identity validation, plus one `PostManifestPublicationRouteCloser` that deduplicates and drains raw connection closure. Purpose-aware anonymous route provisioning and publication-permit scheduling remain separate in the anonymous publisher and its attempt owner; recipient and batch semantics remain publisher-owned, so this does not introduce a generic relay workflow. The structural allocation filter passed all nine cases, and the control and anonymous publisher suites passed all 21 tests in one serialized RSA process.
3. **P2 resolved — admitted acknowledgement facts.** `AdmissionLedger` is now the sole production owner storing `PreSignAcknowledgementSet`, and its effect exports `[TranscriptAcknowledgementValidation]`. The reservation coordinator retains and forwards only those operation artifacts. Runtime rechecks profile, round, and transcript root; `SigningRequestBuilder` additionally checks exact contributor order. After an initial 46-of-48 run exposed two pre-existing two-minute trait flakes, all three tests sharing the unchanged synchronous real-cryptography fixture path received an explicitly approved three-minute trait. The fresh serialized admission/runtime/reservation process passed all 48 tests in 1,406.961 seconds; no production path or fixture authority changed.
4. **P2 — OpalCrypto scalar boundary.** Reopen when OpalCrypto offers the exact additive scalar operation OpalFusion requires. Stop after removing the local scalar implementation with vector parity and no new protocol dependency in OpalCrypto.
5. **P3 — speculative public `Session` and `Mode` names.** Reopen only at an explicit source-breaking release boundary with a concrete public Mosaic composition. Do not add compatibility wrappers or another workflow facade meanwhile.

## Validation Contract

The focused contributor lane and the focused `MosaicMainnetAlphaReservationCoordinatorDependencyValidator` filter each have a 15-second warm post-build budget. The reservation filter must remain statically free of `requireAuthorizationEvaluators()`, evaluator access, `AuthorizationEvaluator.generate()`, real execution-fixture preparation, `LocalContributionMaterial.build`, `makeLocalContributionMaterial`, and `makeMaterializedPreparation`. The alpha-candidate milestone rehearsal remains `./scripts/run-validation-loop.sh mosaic-rehearsal`, serialized in one process with a ten-minute cap. An evaluator-unavailable precondition, SwiftPM process lock, or focused-lane budget violation stops validation rather than triggering per-test retries. No validation command contacts a relay, Tor process, wallet, or BCH node.

For the preceding focused-lane cleanup, `swift build` passed in 0.86 seconds after compilation was warm, test discovery passed, the two focused RSA-free suites passed within their 15-second post-build budget, the static fixture guard passed, and `git diff --check` passed. Its deterministic `all` wrapper was started once, but the runner did not retain its terminal exit status; it is therefore not claimed as passing and was not retried under the bounded no-loop policy. Those serialized real-cryptography conformance and rehearsal results remain historical milestone evidence rather than evidence for later roadmap items.

For the reservation execution-dependency cleanup, the package build passed in 9.63 seconds after Xcode remounted its already-installed matching Metal toolchain, test discovery passed in 29.29 seconds, the material-construction guard passed, and the five-test `MosaicMainnetAlphaReservationCoordinatorDependencyValidator` suite passed in 6.45 seconds with 7.44 seconds of process wall time. The focused filter was run once under its 15-second cap; the serialized full-material suite remains integration coverage rather than part of this fast ownership evidence.

For the route-allocation cleanup, the nine-case structural suite passed and the control plus anonymous publisher filters passed all 21 tests in one serialized RSA process in 185.481 seconds. For the admitted-acknowledgement cleanup, local resolved revisions matched both SwiftPM checkouts, the fresh build passed in 23.26 seconds, and the fresh serialized admission/runtime/reservation process passed all 48 tests in 1,406.961 seconds. No dependency remote, relay, Tor process, wallet, or BCH node was contacted.

The required one-shot `mosaic-rehearsal` then passed both tests in one serialized process on 2026-08-14: conductor completion in 188.344 seconds and contributor exact commit in 132.693 seconds, 2/2 in 321.038 seconds, within the ten-minute cap. This is alpha-candidate milestone evidence over in-memory host and publication seams; it is not live transport, wallet, broadcast, or value-movement evidence.
